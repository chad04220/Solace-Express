Primordia

A warm, shallow sea seen through a microscope. Hundreds of founding microbes compete for sunlight, dissolved nutrients and each other. Every division copies a genome of 32 genes with small errors, so lineages drift, split into new species, form colonies and go extinct as the seasons, currents and climate shift around them.

Watch, nudge, or play god. Feed a struggling lineage, poison a bloom, heat a corner of the sea, stir a current, drop a meteor, engineer a virus against one species, or design your own organism in the Lab and release it.
