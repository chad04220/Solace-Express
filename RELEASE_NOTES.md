## What's new
- Cockpit view on the rasterizer: the cabin comes from its baked mesh and only the moving controls' pixels march the field, and only as deep as the moving parts reach (every cockpit pixel used to march the first 1.2 m of the field: the 72 ms objects pass in the cockpit benchmark). The hull pass writes where each ray leaves the moving volume and the march stops there, in flight as well.
- The airframe's own sun shadow and the sun through the cabin windows come from the airframe's shadow map on the rasterizer; the field is marched only on the map's edge texels and under the moving parts (a per-pixel shadow march was 72 ms of the cockpit frame on the owner's GPU). Rays through the cockpit's moving controls stop where they leave the moving volume, and a march that lands on a surface the mesh already drew is dropped before it is shaded.
- The research craft's camera feeds draw at 0.6 (the front display, the bomb camera) and 0.45 (the side, aft, overhead and floor panels) of the view's pixel density, the front display every frame and the panels in turn within a pixel budget; their display pages redraw at 30 Hz. At full density the XR-40's twelve panels added four to six views' worth of pixels to every frame (49 ms of its 100 ms cockpit frame).
- From Codex's performance review: the terrain grid is indexed (its height and normal samples run once per grid point, not once per triangle corner), the airframe shadow proxy looks up or marches a light's shadow only where the lighting pass will read it (the surface facing the light, inside its cone, lit above the floor), the camera feeds are timed as their own pass (F3, the benchmark, the analysis), and the analysis' per-pixel work counters run on the ray tracer only (they were never the rasterizer's). From its propeller review: the stopped blades' tip fade used an undefined smoothstep on both renderers.
- The research terminal has no button on the main menu any more: it opens on the held combo alone (both sticks pressed on the pad, U and I on the keyboard). Its boot screen is on the screen the instant the combo is held, and holds on its last stage, saying what it is doing, while the previewed craft's shells bake and the launch site's scenery streams in; a bake no longer freezes the screen. The research craft's displays are windows again: the camera feeds (49 ms of the XR-40's frame) are gone except the bomb camera's picture while a bomb falls. The screens' symbology goes with them; the HUD overlay stays.
