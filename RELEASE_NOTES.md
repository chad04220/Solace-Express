## What's new

**XR-11 cockpit**
- One wide curved front display replaces the three front panes, fed by a single new camera at the nose
- Every display is now a true window: each camera looks out exactly where the pilot's eye looks through that display, so the front, side, aft, overhead, chin and floor views line up with each other and with the world
- Displays are as sharp as the screen they're seen on: a 1080p screen's pixel density at the cockpit's field of view, more on larger windows (up to 1440p); the XR-9's displays too
- Every instrument display is drawn at 1080 lines: the research jets' display pages (1080 x 1080 each) and the light aircraft's gauge panels (1080 texels tall)
- The bomb camera's range circles are gone (the impact diamond and range stay)

**Flames and clouds**
- Clouds no longer cover the XR-9's reheat or the XR-11's plasma: the flames are now laid over the clouds behind them instead of under them

**Shadows**
- Air traffic casts real shadows of its own shape (wings, tail, fuselage) instead of round blobs
