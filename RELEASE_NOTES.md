## What's new
- The ray tracer is gone: the game has one renderer, the rasterizer it has defaulted to since v3.18. The Settings renderer choice and the shader-compile screen's "ray tracing" stage are removed ("Ray tracing quality" is now "Render quality"), and the first launch compiles three fewer programs, the ray tracer's being the largest. The README no longer describes a ray-traced renderer.
- The light aircraft's flaps, ailerons, elevators and rudder are now solid moving parts with their own meshes, posed each frame from the controls. The per-pixel march that used to draw them is gone, which makes the outside view of every light aircraft cheaper.
- Fixed: on graphics drivers that strictly validate shaders, the objects pass could draw nothing whenever the airframe had no shadow maps that frame. Wrecks, debris, the UFO, the cloaked XR-40 and the cockpit's moving controls could vanish, for example after a crash or at night without shadow-casting lights.
- The outside of the player's aircraft takes its own shadows (the wing's on the fuselage, the fin's on the tailplane) from the sun shadow map instead of tracing a shadow ray through the airframe for every pixel. In the software reference renderer the aircraft pass is about 40% cheaper in a close chase view, and the image is the same apart from slightly softer shadow edges.
- The XR-40's thruster pods, fans, vectoring vanes, nozzle petals, bomb-bay doors, bomb, laser turrets, elevons and ruddervators, and the XR-30's elevons, canards and rudders, are now solid moving parts with meshes of their own. In chase view these craft no longer trace their moving pieces pixel by pixel.
- Every aircraft body is simplified once it is built. Flat panels become a few large triangles, and curves stay within 1 mm of the true shape (0.4 mm in the cockpit). A light aircraft's outside drops from about 560,000 triangles to about 39,000 and its cockpit from 1.37 million to about 240,000, which cuts the cost of drawing it in every pass, the shadow maps included.
- Building the bodies is quicker. A light aircraft's outside now samples 10 poses instead of 66, the XR-40's 43 instead of 84, and the simplification runs on spare CPU cores while the GPU carries on.
- Fixed: the XR-30 could crash on the runway before moving. Holding full back stick or full roll while parked tipped it onto its tail or a wingtip ("Struck terrain"), because its fly-by-wire commanded rotation with no airspeed at all. On its wheels, its pitch and roll authority now builds up during the takeoff roll and is complete well before rotation speed.
