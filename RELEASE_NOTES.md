## What's new
- From Codex's QA of v3.18.0: a repossession closes the aircraft's airline route and moves the other routes down, so the saves after it load again (the previous backup was never at risk, but the career would not persist from then on); a session that ended inside a job's leg leaves the job waiting at the leg's departure instead of stranded; a diverted job carries its checkpoints, the patient's or the VIP's condition and the ferry charge across legs (the condition no longer resets to perfect and earns an undeserved bonus; the ferry is charged once); Continue no longer starts the leg when its save fails; the autoland reports "stopped past the runway" instead of "complete" when it rolls off the end; `diagnostics.bat` forces the ray tracer with the new `--rt` flag for its comparison (it was timing the rasterizer twice) and every benchmark says which renderer it timed; long button labels shrink to fit.
- Airframe shadow maps for the ground lights too (the apron's and the runway's, which the first version left to the per-pixel march: 13 ms of the night frame), and in cockpit view from the cabin mesh when the exterior one is not baked (10 ms of the cockpit frame). The benchmark window answers Windows between frames, and the research craft's scenes run last in their own file.
- The frame-rate target defaults to 60 fps (it followed the monitor's refresh rate: on a 240 Hz screen the game was asked for 240 fps). Settings still offer the monitor's rate and the other caps.
