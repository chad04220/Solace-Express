## What's new
- From Codex's fleet geometry review (830 studio renders and the production captures): the airframe mesh bake no longer asks the GPU for a texture taller than it allows - the Q400's lattice (22 million points) and the research jets' were refused on a 16k-texture device and the baked airframes came out shredded (missing fuselage, wings and nozzles); the bake now runs in batches within the device's limit and the old meshes are rebaked. The XR-30's thrust-vectoring nozzles are part of the bake's state sweep, so they move with the stick and the hover setting instead of staying frozen at the first frame's angle. The aft-jet nacelles (Starling, XR-10, XR-20) have an open tailpipe with the exhaust cone inside (it was sealed inside the cap). The glass cockpits' overhead console hangs under the overhead plate, lenses visible (they were buried in it).
- `diagnostics.bat` benchmarks full screen at 1920x1080 only (no native-resolution or ray-tracer runs), and a scene whose airframe meshes bake on its first frame keeps answering Windows during the bake (the title says what it is baking) instead of freezing as "Not Responding" after the XR-30 scene.
