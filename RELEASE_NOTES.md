## What's new
- A fifth research craft on the NIGHTGLASS register: the **XR-15 Peregrine**, Codex's FX-27 swept-wing demonstrator on its own hand-written field: a broad chined forebody, a swept wing with ailerons and flaps, twin canted fins with rudders, stabilators, two close-set rear jets with stators, telescopic gear with detailed wheels (tread, hubs, rotors, calipers, tyre-size lettering) behind sliding shutters, and an open single-seat cockpit with a stick, throttle, pedals and three live displays; slate paint with ivory datum panels and copper trim. It fills the tier between the XR-10 and XR-20: 10.5 tonnes, reheat above 85% throttle, Mach 1.4 at height, +11 / -5 g.
- The Peregrine's belly Gatling: Y opens the bay doors and lowers the six-barrel carrier, the trigger (left mouse, Enter, or RB on the pad) spins the rotor up and fires tracer rounds at twelve a second with a little dispersion, a muzzle flash and a gun crack; the rounds fly the same path as the Wraith's lasers and do the same to aircraft, trees, rocks, buildings and the ground. It needs the gear up (GEAR LOCK otherwise), Y safes it (rotor down, carrier up, doors shut), and the HUD has a GATLING tile. Four test cards: supersonic dash, heavy agility, slow and heavy, and a gun run of 60 rounds. Debug scene `research15`.
