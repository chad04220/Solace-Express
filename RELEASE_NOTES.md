## What's new

- **Every aircraft you can fly is on the job screen.** The aircraft list now puts the ones you can fly first, and the
  briefing scrolls (mouse wheel) in its own space above it. Before, a long briefing could push the list off the panel
  and hide the only aircraft able to fly the job (an owned Starling for "The Minister's Jet"), leaving
  "No suitable aircraft".
- **Long settlements scroll.** On the debrief, the lines of a big settlement scroll above the total; they no longer
  run into the total and the Continue button.
- **The sale price is what you get.** The hangar's Sell button showed 70% of the new price; the sale paid less for a
  used or worn aircraft. The button now shows the actual amount, and what's left after a loan on it is repaid.
