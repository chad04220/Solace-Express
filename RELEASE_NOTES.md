## What's new
- The ray tracer is gone: the game has one renderer, the rasterizer it has defaulted to since v3.18. The Settings renderer choice and the shader-compile screen's "ray tracing" stage are removed ("Ray tracing quality" is now "Render quality"), and the first launch compiles three fewer programs, the ray tracer's being the largest. The README no longer describes a ray-traced renderer.
- The light aircraft's flaps, ailerons, elevators and rudder are now solid moving parts with their own meshes, posed each frame from the controls. The per-pixel march that used to draw them is gone, which makes the outside view of every light aircraft cheaper.
- Fixed: on graphics drivers that strictly validate shaders, the objects pass could draw nothing whenever the airframe had no shadow maps that frame. Wrecks, debris, the UFO, the cloaked XR-40 and the cockpit's moving controls could vanish, for example after a crash or at night without shadow-casting lights.
