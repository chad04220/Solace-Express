## What's new
- Resumable jobs: a diversion, a field landing or an abandoned leg closes a leg and the job continues from where the load is (its card is pinned at the top of the contracts tab: continue, release, or practise the approach). Payment comes once, on delivery; the deadline runs across legs. Lessons and checkrides are still flown whole.
- Career changes are transactions: settlements, purchases and sales only count once saved; a failed save is retried from the debrief and the hub and can never pay twice. Saves are version 3 (older saves load). A game closed mid-flight says so at the next start.
- Audio: WASAPI output (shared mode, 10 ms), reopened when the device changes; waveOut stays as the fallback. startup.log names the backend.
- Checkpoints count the closest approach of each frame's movement, so a fast aircraft can't slip through a ring between frames.
- Scoring from the record: touchdown zone, centreline, stable approach, fuel reserve and a shutdown clear of the runway earn a little; floating past the midpoint and landing on fumes cost. Tower compliance: taking off against a hold or landing against a go-around costs pay and reputation; the instruction's state shows beside the recall line.
- There is always a way to earn: when no board job pays and no free lesson is next, a recovery mail run appears (courtesy ride to the field, fair weather, no deadline).
- Options: a field-of-view slider (the cockpit is 19 degrees wider), UI scale, head-look (the cockpit view leans into turns), a blue / orange HUD palette for colour-blind pilots, and the HUD key remembers on / off per camera view.
- Fuel and payload planning: an owned aircraft buys fuel at the departure airport's price (hubs x1, regional x1.15, strips x1.4, snow and gravel x1.8) and keeps what is left; the job card has a fuel choice with the take-off weight against the limit, the roll at that field and the uplift's price; overweight can't dispatch; the hangar refuels. Rentals still come full.
- Resolution scaling: the temporal upscaler reconstructs each output pixel from the nearby render samples (16-point jitter, Gaussian weights with a confidence term); auto resolution now starts at 75% and steps up only with a quarter of the frame budget free.
