## What's new
- Cockpit frame rate: the aircraft is drawn into the depth buffer before anything else, so the terrain, the sea and the scenery behind the cabin walls are thrown away before they are shaded (in the light-aircraft cockpit test view, two thirds of the terrain and sea pixels). The cockpit controls (yokes, pedals, throttles, flap lever, the XR side sticks) are now real moving meshes posed every frame instead of being traced per pixel, and they cast proper shadows in the cabin.
- The XR-40's rudder pedals moved the wrong way: right rudder now pushes the right pedal forward, as in every other aircraft.
- Research terminal: the boot sequence plays to ACCESS GRANTED and holds there (no skip) while every research craft and the launch site are prepared, outside and cockpit, then opens the terminal. Selecting the XR-40 for the first time no longer freezes the game, and the boot screen keeps animating while the aircraft bodies are built. The airframe boxes no longer shrink when the XR-40 is selected.
- Diagnostics v2: the benchmark and the screenshots first build (or load) every aircraft body, outside and cockpit, the research craft's included, and each scene warms until nothing is being built or streamed before it is timed. The cold-start step now reports the shader compile and the time to build every aircraft body separately.
