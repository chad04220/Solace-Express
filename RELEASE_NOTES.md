## What's new

**Fixes**
- Lessons start with the parking brake set, as the instructor says (it was usually off: the brake was only set if the aircraft had settled onto its wheels in the first 0.05 s). The wheel brakes are a little stronger, enough to hold full power on all but the high-thrust STOL types
- The cross-country lesson and the Private Pilot checkride take off into the wind like every other flight (the checkride had a 10 kt tailwind on Harlan Farm's short grass strip); the lessons flown through rings keep their published runway
- The "release the parking brake" message names the key you have it bound to
- The XR-9's research card lists its real airframe limits (+50 / -25 g) instead of "30 g"
- The target marker no longer hides behind the systems panel: the diamond and the off-screen arrow stay in the clear band between the top cards and the instrument panels, drawn over the HUD with the distance / height label on a dark backing
- The "Radio: ..." station label and the radio panel moved below the tower recall panel at the top left
