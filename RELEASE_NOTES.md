## What's new
- Cleaner cockpits: the edges of bezels, frames, window openings, screens and the wing roots seen from the seat now follow the aircraft's exact shape, where before they were jagged.
  - Close to the eye, the mesh pass traces each detail's pixels back onto the true shape.
  - Parts thinner than the mesh grid, such as a wing's trailing edge or a window rim, used to break off ragged. They are now built slightly oversized and trimmed back per pixel. This also removes the pale wedges under the wing roots.
- No more black blotches on the XR-30's screens, and the XR-40's window frames are smooth.
- The cockpit has its own fine sun shadow map, so the window posts and frames no longer look speckled.
- The yokes now turn the right way: rolling right turns the wheel clockwise.
- Twin instrument panels: the radio box no longer overlaps the second engine gauge. The single-engine radio readouts are larger, and all panel displays are sharper at an angle.
- The LED strip under the glareshield no longer runs past the dash.
- The first launch rebuilds every aircraft's mesh once.
