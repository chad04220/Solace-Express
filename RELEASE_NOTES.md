## What's new
- Retractable landing gear on low-wing aircraft (the Starling 500, Swift S6, Osprey C6, XR-10 and XR-20) now folds sideways. Each main leg swings inboard about a fore-and-aft hinge until it lies along the wing, and the wheel ends flat in a streamlined fairing under the wing root, behind closing doors. Before, the wheels rose straight up through wings thinner than the wheels are tall. On the Swift S6, Starling 500 and XR-10 the inboard flap now starts just outboard of that fairing, so a lowered flap never cuts through the folded gear.
- Gear wells are now open when the gear is down. A modelling slip had left a thin skin across every bay opening, so the doors hung beside a closed belly.
- The landing gear, the nose and tail wheels and the gear doors of every aircraft built from the shared model are solid moving parts. A light aircraft's body now builds from a single pose, so first-time loading is quicker, and nothing on the airframe is traced pixel by pixel any more.
