## What's new
- Resumable jobs: a diversion, a field landing or an abandoned leg closes a leg and the job continues from where the load is (its card is pinned at the top of the contracts tab: continue, release, or practise the approach). Payment comes once, on delivery; the deadline runs across legs. Lessons and checkrides are still flown whole.
- Career changes are transactions: settlements, purchases and sales only count once saved; a failed save is retried from the debrief and the hub and can never pay twice. Saves are version 3 (older saves load). A game closed mid-flight says so at the next start.
- Audio: WASAPI output (shared mode, 10 ms), reopened when the device changes; waveOut stays as the fallback. startup.log names the backend.
- Checkpoints count the closest approach of each frame's movement, so a fast aircraft can't slip through a ring between frames.
