## What's new
- The flight HUD moved to the edges of the screen: a slim band across the top (mission, heading tape, clock, autopilot), a speed tape down the left and an altitude tape down the right (stall band, Vref, trend, radar height, vertical speed), the attitude ball with the approach needles, wind and g in the bottom-left corner, the systems as tiles in the bottom-right, the tower's last call and the messages on the rails, the instructor's hint along the bottom. STALL, PULL UP, GEAR and ENGINE OFF are icons under the band, and a stall or terrain warning lights the screen's edges. Every arrow is a filled dart or a tapered arrow with an outline.
- Voices for everything the game added since the last pack: the failures (engine failure with the glide ratio and best glide speed, power loss per engine, alternator and battery, pitot, gear stuck up or down, flap asymmetry, icing), the research test cards (each card's intro, every step and the sign-off), aerobatics figures, the new craft names (Swift, Osprey, XR-10 Nightjar, XR-20 Mantis), rain and snow, the wind-shift runway change and the belly landing rating. Four research lines that still named the old craft numbers were re-recorded. The airport and weather announcements at the start of a flight are spoken again (they were cleared with the previous flight's calls), a mechanical engine failure gets glide guidance instead of restart advice, a failure interrupts a routine tower call, and pod settings coalesce like flaps. Tests now load every one of the 1068 clips and resolve every card, failure and craft line.
