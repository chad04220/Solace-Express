## What's new
- New freelance work: medevacs (a patient who must get to a hospital fast and gently, their condition on the HUD), VIP charters (a comfort meter and a tip for a limousine ride), night freight between lit fields (landing light on for the touchdown), low-visibility runs under a low overcast (be lined up when you break out, or go around) and surveys (six checkpoints flown at one altitude, paid by the share held in the band). Each names its rules on the job card.
- Research register performance pass: the four craft now sit in clear tiers (XR-10 just subsonic at Mach 0.98, XR-20 with reheat to Mach 2, XR-30 Mach 2.8, XR-40 Mach 4.4), each more agile than the last, with their own structural limits (+9/-4, +14/-6, +40/-20, +90/-45 g). An airframe pushed past its limit takes a short overstress before it fails instead of breaking at once.
- Research test cards: each craft has sorties in the terminal that push it to its numbers and exercise what is unique to it (dashes, climbs, pulls, rolls, slow flight and stalls, the Wraith's hover, vertical landing, cloak, lasers and bombs). The HUD tracks the step; a finished card is signed off and remembered.
- The research register is renumbered with room to grow: XR-10 Nightjar, XR-20 Mantis, XR-30 Specter, XR-40 Wraith (the terminal, the HUD, the harness scene names research10 / research20 / research40 and the aircraft ids follow).
- Dynamic weather: about a third of freelance jobs come with a forecast, and the weather moves during the flight (the wind backs or veers and strengthens, cloud and visibility change, rain or snow sets in). The job card shows the forecast and warns of a wind shift; the tower reads the wind as it is; on the autopilot the approach is re-planned for the other runway end when the wind has swung, while there is room to.
- Menus by keyboard and gamepad: the arrow keys and the D-pad move a focus ring between buttons, Enter / Space / A press one; the mouse or the stick takes over the moment it moves.
- Tower calls that events have overtaken (a go-around, a runway change, a phase gone by) are dropped instead of being read out late; after a pause the recall line says the instruction was given before it.
- The research terminal has a main-menu entry: open once the campaign is flown, before that behind a reminder that nothing there counts.
